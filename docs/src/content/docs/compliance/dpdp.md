## What is DPDP 2023?

The **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** (DPDP) is India's landmark
data privacy law.

**Key deadline: Rules are expected to be notified by May 2025,
with compliance required within 1 year of notification.**

## How IndusBase Helps

IndusBase Studio includes a built-in **DPDP Compliance Dashboard** at `/dpdp`.
